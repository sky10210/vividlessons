Find Your Housing Guardrails

Calculate before you browse.

Two useful guardrails: keep rent near or below 30% of gross income, and keep the full housing cost—rent, basic utilities, renters insurance, and required monthly fees—near or below 35% of estimated take-home pay. These are planning guidelines, not promises that a budget will work.

2. Why must you calculate total housing cost instead of looking only at rent? (5 points)
True monthly housing cost = rent + monthly utilities + renters insurance + required monthly fees

Do not add the security deposit here. A deposit is a one-time move-in cost, not a recurring monthly cost.

Read the Lease Before You Sign

Focus on the clauses that can cost you money or restrict what you can do.

Classroom sample: This shortened lease is fictional and designed for practice. A real lease may contain additional state and local requirements.
1. TERM AND RENEWAL. The lease runs August 1, 2026 through July 31, 2027. It converts to month-to-month unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the end date.
2. RENT AND LATE FEE. Monthly rent is due on the 1st. If full payment is not received by 5:00 p.m. on the 5th, the tenant owes a $50 late fee plus $10 for each additional calendar day, up to $100 total for that month.
3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The $1,000 deposit may be used for unpaid rent, cleaning beyond ordinary turnover, or tenant-caused damage beyond normal wear. The tenant should document move-in condition in writing and with photographs.
4. UTILITIES AND INSURANCE. Landlord pays water, sewer, and trash. Tenant pays electricity and internet and must maintain renters insurance during the lease.
5. OCCUPANCY, GUESTS, AND PETS. Only named tenants may occupy the unit. Guests staying more than 7 consecutive nights require written approval. No pet is permitted without written approval, a $300 pet deposit, and $35 monthly pet rent.
6. ENTRY AND REPAIRS. Except for emergencies, the landlord will give at least 24 hours’ notice before entry. The tenant must promptly report leaks, unsafe conditions, or needed repairs and may not deduct repair costs from rent without written authorization.
7. EARLY TERMINATION. A tenant who leaves early remains responsible for rent until the unit is re-rented or the lease ends and must also pay a $600 early-termination charge, except where law provides otherwise.
