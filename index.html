Simple for teachers. Clear for students.

Can I try a lesson before buying?

Yes. Three short interactive previews are free and require no account.

The planned classroom-code experience will not require individual student accounts. This paid access system is still being built.

The planned full-module workflow ends with a personalized certificate that students can save and submit through their school's learning management system. Certificate generation is not yet live.

Can I purchase a module today?

Not yet. Stripe checkout and secure classroom access are being prepared. The free previews are available now.
